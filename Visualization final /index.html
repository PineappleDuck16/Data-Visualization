Exploring America's National Parks

A journey through the history of the U.S. National Parks System

The Birth of National Parks

The United States National Park System began with a simple yet revolutionary idea: preserve natural wonders for future generations.

The Hayden Expedition

In 1871, the Hayden Expedition surveyed the Yellowstone region, documenting its geysers, hot springs, and unique landscape. Their reports and photographs by William Henry Jackson convinced Congress to protect this land.

Yellowstone: The First National Park

On March 1, 1872, President Ulysses S. Grant signed the Yellowstone National Park Protection Act, creating the world's first national park. This historic move set aside over 2 million acres of public land "for the benefit and enjoyment of the people."

The Growth of a National Treasure
